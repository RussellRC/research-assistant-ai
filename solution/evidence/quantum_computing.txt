## Research Findings

Quantum Error Correction (QEC) is essential for developing fault-tolerant quantum computers, addressing the inherent susceptibility of qubits to noise and decoherence. Recent advancements span experimental validation, novel theoretical code development, and sophisticated decoding algorithms, all contributing to the goal of scalable and robust quantum systems. A significant stride is the experimental demonstration of logical qubits outperforming their physical counterparts in terms of coherence times or error rates, notably by Google and in trapped-ion systems, moving QEC beyond the 'break-even' point. Concurrently, research is actively developing more efficient QEC codes beyond the surface code, such as Low-Density Parity-Check (LDPC) codes for reduced qubit overhead, Floquet codes, and bosonic codes tailored to specific hardware.
